Welcome to Littlegill

Some places just let you breathe out.

Littlegill is our small stone cottage beside its own little gill, kept simple and comfortable for four people and a well behaved dog. Light the wood burner, open the windows, and let the valley do the rest. There is no reception desk and no checkout queue, just a warm house that is yours for the week.

  • Two bedrooms
  • Wood burner
  • Dog friendly
  • Wi-Fi
