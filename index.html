Welcome to Littlegill

Some places just let you breathe out.

Littlegill is our light, modern house in the hills, named after the little gill nearby. Five bedrooms, four bathrooms and a garden with the fells beyond the fence, so there is room for the whole family, two families who get on well, or a walking party with a lot of boots. No reception desk, no checkout queue, just a warm house that is yours for the stay.
