The House

Light, modern and made for a full house

Five bedrooms, four bathrooms and space for everyone to spread out.

shoes off, kettle on

Room to be together, room to escape

Littlegill is a detached house that works as hard for one big family as it does for two families sharing or a walking party. Downstairs flows from kitchen to dining to sitting room and out to the garden. Upstairs, five bedrooms and a stack of bathrooms mean nobody is negotiating over showers.

Sitting room

Room for everyone to land

Three big sofas, a smart TV and windows that frame the hills. There is space for the whole party to pile in for a film, and enough corners for the ones who would rather read.

the hill view comes free with the sofa
Kitchen & utility

A kitchen that keeps up

A modern kitchen with room to cook for nine without elbowing anyone. Next door, the utility room takes the washing machine, the boots and the wet coats, so the rest of the house stays calm.

big breakfasts are non-negotiable
Dining

One table, all of you

The dining area sits between the kitchen and the garden doors, which makes it the natural home for long dinners, board games and the jigsaw somebody always starts. When the sun plays along, dinner moves out to the deck.

doors open, dinner outside
Main bedroom

Soft landings

A calm double with proper bedding, a chaise for slow mornings and countryside views from the window. Close the door and the rest of the house disappears.

first one up gets the quiet
Up in the eaves

The room everyone bags first

A double tucked under the roofline with its own TV and a walk-in dressing room next door. It feels like a little suite of its own, which is why it goes first.

Bedrooms

Five rooms, everyone sorted

Alongside the main and attic rooms there are three more bedrooms: a navy double, a bright pink double and a single that younger guests tend to claim. Proper beds all round, no sofa beds, no camp beds, no arguments.

kids pick their rooms at the door
Bathrooms

Four bathrooms, zero queueing

A family bathroom with a proper bath, walk-in showers, and a downstairs loo for muddy-boot emergencies. With four bathrooms across the house, everyone gets ready at the same time and nobody hammers on a door.

hot water that means it

Supper outside when it's fine

An enclosed garden with a big lawn, planted borders and a decked terrace with table and chairs. The fence keeps little ones in, and the hills carry on where it stops.

Good to know

The practical bits

Arrival and departure

Check-in is from 4pm and check-out is by 10am. Full directions and entry details are sent with your booking confirmation, and we are always at the end of the phone if you get turned around in the lanes.

What's included

All bedding and towels, Wi-Fi throughout, a fully equipped kitchen and a utility room with washing machine. Just bring yourselves, food and boots.

Dogs

Dogs are welcome by arrangement. Add yours when you book, or drop us a line first if you are not sure, and we will let you know anything you need.

Children and infants

Children are very welcome, and the enclosed garden earns its keep with little ones. If you need a travel cot or highchair, just mention it when you book.

A few house rules

No smoking anywhere in the house, and the quiet setting means no parties or loud music outdoors. Littlegill is a home, not a venue, and we like to think it feels that way.
