The Cottage

Small, warm and properly comfortable

Everything you need for four people and a dog, nothing shouting for attention.

shoes off, fire on

Kept simple on purpose

Littlegill is a traditional stone cottage that we have looked after gently rather than made over. Thick walls, deep windowsills, warm floors and honest furniture. It suits couples with the second bedroom spare, two couples who get on well, or a small family with wellies.

  • Sleeps four
  • Two bedrooms
  • Wood burner
  • Dog friendly
  • Wi-Fi
  • Parking
Sitting room

The room the day ends in

A deep sofa, two armchairs, the wood burner and a basket of logs to get you started. There is a smart TV if the weather truly beats you, board games in the dresser, and a shelf of books other guests have loved and left.

the dog basket lives by the fire
Kitchen & dining

A kitchen that actually cooks

Full sized oven and hob, dishwasher, fridge freezer, decent pans and knives that cut. The table seats four comfortably, six at a squeeze. We leave tea, fresh milk and a little welcome bake for your arrival.

the bread bin is never empty for long
Main bedroom

Wake up to the valley

A king size bed with proper cotton bedding, blackout curtains for long lie-ins and a window seat for slow mornings. The morning light in here is the reason half our guests come back.

best sunrise in the house
Second bedroom

Twins or a super king, your call

Made up as twin beds or zipped together as a super king, just tell us when you book. There is a full wardrobe, a reading lamp each and the same view of the garden.

kids tend to claim this one
Bathroom

Hot water that means it

A bath with a shower over, warm towels on the rail and generous hot water after a day on the fells. Towels are provided, including a stack of dog towels by the back door.

muddy boots stop at the porch
Garden

Breakfast outside when it's fine

An enclosed garden with a table, chairs and a bench that catches the evening sun. The gill runs along the bottom boundary, which is exactly as soothing as it sounds. Dogs love it, children dam the stream.

listen for the water, it never stops
Good to know

The practical bits

Arrival and departure

Check-in is from 4pm and check-out is by 10am. Full directions and entry details are sent with your booking confirmation, and we are always at the end of the phone if you get turned around in the lanes.

What's included

All bedding and towels, logs to start you off, Wi-Fi, tea and coffee, and a welcome tray for your first evening. Electricity and heating are included in the price, whatever the weather does.

Dogs

Up to two well behaved dogs are very welcome. We provide dog towels, a spare basket and a list of our favourite dog friendly pubs. We just ask that dogs stay off the beds and are not left alone in the cottage.

Children and infants

Children are welcome. A travel cot and highchair are available on request at no charge, just mention it when you book. The garden is enclosed but the gill is open water, so little ones need watching outside.

A few house rules

No smoking anywhere in the cottage, and the quiet valley means no parties or loud music outdoors. Littlegill is a home, not a venue, and we like to think it feels that way.
