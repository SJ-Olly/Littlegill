Before you book

Questions we're often asked

Can we come for a short break?

Yes. Minimum stays vary through the year and show automatically when you pick dates in the calendar. Midweek breaks are often the quietest and best value.

What time can we arrive and leave?

Check-in is from 4pm and check-out is by 10am, which gives us time to make the house lovely between guests. If the house is free either side of your stay we will always try to be flexible, just ask.

Can we bring our dog?

Dogs are welcome by arrangement. Add yours when you book, or drop us a line first if you are not sure, and we will let you know anything you need.

What if we need to cancel?

Our cancellation terms are shown clearly before you confirm your booking. If your plans change, tell us as soon as you can and we will do our best to help, including moving dates where possible.

Is there anything we need to bring?

Just yourselves, food and boots. Bedding, towels, Wi-Fi and a fully equipped kitchen are all included.
