Location & Things to Do

Boots on at the doorstep

Walks from the door, a pub down the lane and skies full of stars.

the best days start slowly here

A quiet valley that keeps you busy

Littlegill sits in quiet hill country where sheep outnumber people comfortably. You can fill a week without moving the car, or use the house as a base for bigger days out. Either way, the kettle is waiting when you get back.

Walking

Riverside strolls, ridge lines and proper hill days, all starting from the door. We keep a folder of favourite routes in the house, graded from gentle to heroic.

ask us for the secret waterfall walk

The village

A gentle stroll down the lane brings you to a good pub, a better bakery and a shop that covers the essentials. The pub does a proper Sunday roast and welcomes dogs.

book the roast, trust us

Days out

Market towns, castles, gardens and steam railways are all within easy reach for a day trip. We will happily plan a route over a cup of tea before you set off.

market day is worth the drive
Evenings & rainy days

Dark skies and slow afternoons

There is almost no light pollution here, so clear nights put on a show that city visitors do not quite believe. Rainy days are for films, board games and the bakery run. Nobody has ever complained.

bring a big coat and look up
Getting here

Easier to find than you'd think

The house is reached by a quiet lane with private parking on the drive. The nearest railway station is a short taxi ride away, and we can arrange a local taxi to meet your train if you are travelling without a car.

Full directions, the postcode and a what three words location are sent with your booking confirmation, along with our number in case the satnav gets creative in the lanes.
